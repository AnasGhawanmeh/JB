| File | Purpose |
|---|---|

Rebuild everything with: `cd scripts && python model.py && python make_figures.py && node build_docx.js`

## Things only you can do

1. **Permission.** Get written permission from the Ministry of Energy and Minerals (and SEURECA, if a contract applies) before submitting. Then complete the "Data permission" paragraph on the title page.
2. **Metered data.** These replace the three assumptions that dominate the remaining uncertainty. Each change is one line in `model.py`, and the paper rebuilds automatically.
   - **Refinery crude intake** by refinery: replaces `eta_ref` and settles H1 versus H2.
   - **Oil-field gas** split into reinjection and fuel: replaces `own_comb_share`.
   - **Power-and-water plant fuel**: replaces `eta_pp`.
3. **Zenodo.** Upload `data/` together with `scripts/`, then replace `10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX` in `scripts/content.js` (Data availability section) and rebuild.
4. **Authors.** Fill in the title page and `CITATION.cff`.
5. **Target journal.** Energy Strategy Reviews. Elsevier accepts any consistent reference style at first submission; the references are in Harvard style.

## Assumptions to defend in review (all in Table 4 and varied in the Monte Carlo)

| Assumption | Why it is defensible |
|---|---|
| Refinery net efficiency 0.95 (0.92–0.98) | Refinery fuel and losses are 4–8% of throughput (Szklo and Schaeffer, 2007) |
| H1: refinery gap sits in the crude export series (Duqm is in a special economic zone) | Over-recorded output (H0) is rejected by the data; H2 is reported alongside |
| 15% of oil-field gas combusted (0–30%) | Matches EI gas consumption and GCP gas CO₂ to within 3–4% |
| Fleet efficiency 45% (40–50%) | CCGT-dominated fleet; the remainder is reported as "desalination and unattributed" |
| 80% of naphtha/other residual is non-energy use (60–100%) | Petrochemical feedstock at Sohar, plus bitumen and lubricants |
| 90% of retail jet fuel is international (80–95%) | IRES bunker convention |
