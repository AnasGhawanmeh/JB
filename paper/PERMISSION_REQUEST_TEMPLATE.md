# Request for permission to publish (template)

To: Director, National Energy Balance Unit, Ministry of Energy and Minerals, Sultanate of Oman
Cc: National Centre for Statistics and Information
Date: [date]

**Subject: Request for permission to publish a peer-reviewed article based on 2025 national energy statistics**

Dear [Name],

I am writing to request the Ministry's written permission to submit a manuscript to an international peer-reviewed journal (*Energy Strategy Reviews*, Elsevier). The manuscript is titled "Closing the books of a rentier energy economy: reconciled energy accounts, CO₂ drivers and the renewable dual dividend in Oman, 2015–2025".

The manuscript uses aggregate 2025 energy statistics, compiled in the course of the National Energy Balance work, as follows:

1. Only national aggregates are used: production, trade, transformation, sectoral consumption and plant-level renewable generation. No company-confidential data, contracts or prices are disclosed.
2. The manuscript identifies data issues and proposes improvements to the statistical system. These relate to refinery intake metering, the reporting of gas reinjection, and the fuel split of power-and-water plants.
3. A public data package will contain only the aggregate figures that appear in the paper.

We would welcome the Ministry's review of the manuscript before submission. We would also be glad to acknowledge the Ministry, or to include Ministry co-authors, as you consider appropriate.
